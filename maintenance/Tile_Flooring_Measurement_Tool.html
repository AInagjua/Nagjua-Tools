Flats & Sections

Each card below is one flat. Inside a flat, add a Section for each named group on your sheet (e.g. "Hall Flooring", "Master Bathroom Wall Tiles", "Skirting") - each section gets its own rows and its own Total Quantity. Mark a row SF (Length × Breadth) or RF (Length only, for skirting/borders/sills), and Add or Deduct (deductions are window/door openings subtracted from that section's total).
